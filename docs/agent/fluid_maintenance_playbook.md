# Fluid Maintenance

Use this route for oils, coolants, brake and steering fluids, transmission,
transfer-case, differential and AWD-coupling service.

## Decide From The Unit, Not The Product

Identify enough of the vehicle and serviced unit to separate materially
different variants: market, engine or transmission code, drivetrain and the
actual operation. Distinguish service refill from dry fill, oil-only from
oil-and-filter, simple drain from pan/cooler work, and any temperature or
level-setting procedure.

Choose evidence by risk and availability. Exact-market OEM service information
is strongest; licensed repair databases can fill gaps, and lubricant selectors
help map a confirmed approval to products. Mercedes-Benz and ZF fluid references
are useful for approval families, but do not alone prove a vehicle variant or
fill quantity. Several sites repeating one source are still one source.

If exact data is unavailable, give the useful part that is supported and name
the remaining check. Do not turn a selector suggestion into confirmed fitment
or block a low-risk preliminary answer merely to satisfy a fixed source count.

## Useful Answer

State the vehicle/unit and service operation used, required specification or
approval, the supported quantity or range with its fill context, evidence
quality, and the one check that could change the answer. A safety-critical or
variant-sensitive procedure needs exact OEM/licensed confirmation before work.

Use raw read-only discovery for `recommend_automotive_sources` with
`data_type="fluids"` when the routed documents are insufficient.
