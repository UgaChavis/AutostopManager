# Business Documents

Use the CRM print module and standard AutoStop templates; choose the current CRM flow from available card context, not parallel PDF or HTML.

Manual completion acts use `save_completion_act_form`; `reset_completion_act_form` needs separate authority and a verified recovery snapshot.

Take requisites from a current confirmed source; old contracts and tenders are history, not current details. Say when evidence is missing and keep private details out of Git.

Before external delivery, verify relevant facts and calculations and inspect meaningful rendered pages or sheets. For regulated forms, use current official requirements; parsed text is not render proof. Keep output private and report only checks actually made.
